Rules & Policy

To ensure a smooth and delightful experience for everyone, please take a moment to read through our guidelines. By placing an order, you agree to the following terms.

01

Ordering Policy

Orders must be placed at least 2–3 days before the desired pickup or delivery date. Customers must provide complete and accurate order details, including quantity, flavor, design (if applicable), and contact information. Orders are subject to availability of ingredients and production capacity.

02

Payment Policy

A down payment may be required to confirm large or customized orders. Full payment must be completed before pickup or delivery unless otherwise agreed upon. Payments can be made through approved payment methods only.

03

Cancellation and Changes

Order cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the scheduled pickup or delivery. Changes to orders may only be accepted if requested before production begins. Deposits for customized orders may be non-refundable.

04

Pickup and Delivery Policy

Customers must arrive on time for scheduled pickups. Delivery fees may apply depending on the location. The seller is not responsible for damages once the order has been received by the customer.

05

Product Quality and Safety

All baked goods are prepared following proper food safety and hygiene standards. Customers should inform the seller of any food allergies before placing an order. Products should be consumed within the recommended period for best quality and freshness.

06

Refund Policy

Refunds or replacements will only be considered for orders that are incorrect, damaged before receipt, or do not meet the agreed specifications. Complaints must be reported within 24 hours of receiving the order, along with proof if necessary.

07

Customer Conduct

Customers are expected to communicate respectfully when placing orders or raising concerns. Any fraudulent transactions or false claims may result in refusal of future orders.

🍪 By placing an order, the customer acknowledges and agrees to follow these rules and policies. 🍪
