All About Us

How Lil' n bites came to be.

It all started as a joke. My cousin Princess Erica Bueno and I were just talking one day, laughing at the thought: “What if we started a baked goods business?”

But the more we talked about it, the more it didn’t sound so crazy. So we started brainstorming—going through a selection of names, sketching logo concepts, and imagining what our little brand could look like.

Then one day, without planning, without a perfect recipe, without knowing what the outcome would be—we just went for it. We started from scratch, accepted orders without hesitating, and trusted the process.

We started July 2025. Back then, our cookies came out flat—but now, I think we are improving. We also had our first ever pop-up booth, and we can say that it was a fun and worth-it experience. Those sleepless nights making dough were crazy, yet they became some of the most memorable moments for us.

We are praying and hoping to expand more, to have more different treats. We are doing our best—me and my ate Princess Erica.

We are hoping that you all continue to support our business. If we can turn our jokes and imaginations into reality, I know you can do it too. Never stop dreaming and believe in yourself always.

God bless us all.

From jokes, turned to reality. 🍪
